Copy for a project showcase page or other listing. It's for people who might use sayit; the README is for building it and contributing. Keep it in step with the README when features change.

## Name and tagline

**sayit**: private dictation that never leaves your computer.

## Short description (80 characters or fewer)

Hold a key, speak, release: your words appear. Local, private, and light.

## Long description

sayit turns your voice into text in any app. Hold a key, speak, and let go: the words are typed wherever your cursor is, whether that's an email, a document, a chat or a code editor.

Everything happens on your computer. The speech model runs on the CPU, with no GPU and no internet connection needed once it's downloaded. Your audio is held in memory only while you speak and is never saved. sayit has no account, no sign-up and no telemetry, and the app contains no networking code at all.

It's built for everyday laptops: about 1 GB of memory while the model is loaded, and typically a fraction of a second per sentence on an entry-level 8 GB machine.

sayit is free and open source (MIT OR Apache-2.0). Anyone can read the code, check the privacy claims and build it themselves.

## Key features

- **Push-to-talk anywhere:** hold a hotkey (Right Option by default), or press once to start and again to stop.
- **Private by design:** no network code, no telemetry, audio never touches the disk.
- **Light:** runs on the CPU, about 1 GB of RAM, typically 0.1–0.3 s per sentence.
- **Choice of speech models:** Parakeet v2 (English) or v3 (25 European languages), or Moonshine Medium and Small (English) for smaller downloads.
- **Clean text:** drops "um" and "uh", and puts your clipboard back after pasting.
- **Menu-bar app with a setup assistant:** model download, permissions checklist, microphone and hotkey choice.
- **Optional clipboard history**, kept in memory only.
- **Free and open source.**

## Platform

macOS 13 or later. Linux support is written but untested.
