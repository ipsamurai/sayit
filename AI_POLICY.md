# AI policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-25_

## AI-assisted development

Much of sayit's code and documentation was written with the help of AI coding tools, working under ipsamurai's direction. ipsamurai decided what sayit does and how it's built, reviewed and tested the changes, and chose what went in. ipsamurai is the author and rights holder of this work (see [NOTICE](NOTICE)). AI assistance doesn't change that, or the license sayit is offered under.

Commits made with an AI tool's help say so in a `Co-Authored-By:` line.

## Contributions made with AI tools

AI-assisted pull requests are welcome on the same terms as any other. If you use an AI tool:

- **You're the author.** You must understand, review and test every line you submit, and be able to explain it in review.
- **You're responsible for it**, including making sure it doesn't copy code whose license is incompatible with MIT OR Apache-2.0.
- **Follow the ground rules** in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). They apply unchanged: no network code, no stored audio or transcripts, least privilege.
- Mention the AI help in the pull request, or with a `Co-Authored-By:` line.

## AI inside the app

sayit uses a speech-recognition model (see the README's [Speech model](README.md#speech-model) section) to turn your voice into text. It runs entirely on your computer. sayit doesn't use generative AI or chatbots, and it sends nothing to any AI service or anywhere else. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

## Using sayit

sayit is open source under MIT OR Apache-2.0. Those licenses let anyone use, study, change and share it, for any purpose, and this policy doesn't add any restrictions to them. You're responsible for how you use sayit, including through scripts or other automation you set up; see [DISCLAIMER.md](DISCLAIMER.md).
